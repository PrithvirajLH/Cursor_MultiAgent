## 4. Decisions and assumptions

1. **The payload carries `agentDisplayName`, not an actor id.** The display name is
   all the From line needs, and it keeps the processor from doing a user lookup on
   every send. `NotificationsService` already has the actor.
2. **The team rule lives in `NotificationsService`, not in the util.** For teams
   that should stay anonymous, simply **do not put the name on the payload** —
   `buildFromIdentity` then returns the generic identity by itself. One decision,
   one place, and the util stays dumb.
3. **Which teams are generic is config, not code:**
   `EMAIL_GENERIC_IDENTITY_TEAMS`, comma-separated **slugs**, default
   `hr,payroll`. The owner's decision named HR and Payroll for termination work,
   but that is a policy that will change and should not need a deploy.
4. **A `PROCESSING` row older than 10 minutes is reclaimed to `PENDING`** by the
   sweeper. Without this, a row that was mid-send when the app restarted is stuck
   forever in a status nothing looks at. Ten minutes is comfortably longer than
   any real send. **Respect `attempts`** — a reclaimed row that has exhausted its
   attempts goes to `FAILED`, not round again.
5. **The sweeper ships enabled**, `EMAIL_OUTBOX_SWEEP_ENABLED` (default on),
   `EMAIL_OUTBOX_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS` (default 60000), batch of 20 per tick. Unlike
   retention, its failure mode is *losing* mail rather than deleting it, so off-by-
   default would be the wrong caution. It is a no-op when nothing is pending, and
   while SMTP is unconfigured every attempt terminates immediately anyway.
6. **Build the counts before enabling the sweeper.** Nobody knows how many
   `PENDING` or `PROCESSING` rows production is carrying. Ship the counts, look,
   *then* judge whether the first sweep needs supervising. Report the production
   numbers — the planner will want them.
7. **No new endpoint for the counts.** They go on the existing
   `/api/health/ready` and the existing `GET /api/operations` snapshot.

## 7. Security considerations

- **Do not weaken any 1.22 guard to make this easier.** The internal-note
  refusal, the domain allowlist and the no-reply refusal all have tests; they
  stay green.
- **The counts are numbers.** No address, no subject, no body on
  `/api/health/ready` or the operations snapshot.
- **The sweeper must not resurrect a `FAILED` row.** `'SMTP not configured'` and a
  suppressed address are deliberately terminal. Retrying them would send mail
  somebody decided should not be sent.
- **`agentDisplayName` becomes visible to requesters.** That is the owner's
  decision, and `EMAIL_GENERIC_IDENTITY_TEAMS` is the escape hatch — which is
  exactly why it is config rather than a constant.
- The sweeper runs while the pilot switch is on, so anything it retries goes to
  the owner. Fine today; worth remembering it is the thing that will send the
  backlog the moment the pilot list is cleared.
