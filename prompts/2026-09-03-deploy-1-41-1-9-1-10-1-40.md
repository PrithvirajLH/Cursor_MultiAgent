```bash

DATABASE_URL="$PROD" DIRECT_URL="$PROD" npx prisma migrate status   # expect 54
```

  deploys.

## 6. Known and deliberate — do not "fix" these

- **A looped-in EMPLOYEE can reply by email but cannot open the ticket** (403).
  Card 1.36 grants an EMPLOYEE only tickets they requested, and following does not
  change that. A loop-in is an **email-only participant**. Intended.
- **A stranger's inbound reply answers 201**, not an error. Deliberate: an error
  makes the sender's mail server retry forever. The attempt is recorded as an
  event and the body discarded.
- **A follow-up on an unassigned ticket notifies nobody** and stays set, with a
  warning logged. Clearing it would throw the reminder away silently; left set it
  keeps surfacing in "Follow-ups due today".
- **Staff still receive per-event emails.** Card 1.42 removes them; it is not in
  this deploy.
- **`e2e/` is untracked on purpose.** Leave it out of the package.
