## ⚠️ After the deploy, expect the three existing email tickets to STILL look wrong

**This is the most likely way somebody concludes the deploy failed when it
didn't.** Read this before checking anything.

The planner read the stored bodies: **two messages of 5,472 and 5,325 characters,
both HTML, and in neither is our marker on a line of its own.** That matters
because of how the two halves work:

- **Fault A converts at the Graph boundary** — so it only ever affects **mail that
  arrives after this deploy.** It does not rewrite anything already stored, by
  design: storage stays whole.
- **Fault B trims at display time**, so it *does* apply to stored messages — **but
  its markers are line-anchored (`^…$`), and in these two stored bodies the marker
  sits inside `<p>…</p>`.** So B cannot match them either.

**Net: the three email tickets already in production will look exactly as bad
after this deploy as before it.** That is correct behaviour, not a failure.

- [ ] ⚠️ **So do NOT verify this by opening an existing ticket.** Verify it with a
      **new** inbound email.
- [ ] **If the owner wants the three existing ones cleaned up**, that is a one-off
      backfill and a separate decision — the conversion would have to be applied to
      stored bodies, which is the one thing card 1.62 deliberately does not do.
      **Do not improvise it during a deploy.**
